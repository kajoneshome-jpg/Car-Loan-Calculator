How this looks in your pay each week

Paydays with the same repayments are grouped together.

Family loans: $10,414 in total, repaid from Thu 19 Nov 2026. The repayment agreements set $500 a week, with a last payment of $414 on Thu 8 Apr 2027. If a payment slides, the agreements provide for a replacement Schedule 1 and these dates would move.

How the sums work: the loan establishment fee is added to the amount borrowed. Interest is worked out weekly at the yearly rate ÷ 52, and the weekly repayment is the amount that pays the loan off exactly over the term. The first car repayment comes out of the first Thursday payday at least a week after the purchase date. Lenders usually charge interest monthly or fortnightly, so a real quote will differ slightly.

Paying extra: paying more than the weekly repayment finishes the loan sooner and saves interest, but only if the loan allows extra repayments and early payout without a fee. Check this before signing.
